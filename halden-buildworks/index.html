    Before you ask

    Straight answers

    The questions we hear on almost every first site visit.

    Is the price really fixed?

    Yes, once the drawings and specification are agreed. It changes only if you change the scope, or if the ground hides something no survey could have shown, such as an unrecorded well. Either way you approve a written variation before any extra work starts.

    How do payments work?

    In stages tied to milestones you can see: 10% at contract, then ground slab, top out, weathertight and practical completion. We hold back 2.5% until the end of the defects period.

    Do you handle permits and building control?

    Yes. We prepare and submit building control applications, book every inspection and keep the sign off certificates for your handover pack. Planning permission itself stays with your architect, and we can introduce one if you need it.

    Can we stay open or live at home during the works?

    Usually. Fit outs are often built at night or in phases so you keep trading. For extensions we seal off the work area and keep one kitchen and one bathroom working for all but a few planned days.

    What insurance and warranties do you carry?

    Public liability of $10 million, employer's liability and contract works cover on every job. New homes carry a 10 year structural warranty. Certificates are sent with every proposal.
